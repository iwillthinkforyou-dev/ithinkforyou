Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. What This Policy Covers

This Privacy Policy explains how I THINK FOR YOU collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use this website, contact us, submit a story, or voluntarily make a contribution.

2. Information You May Provide

Depending on how you use the website, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name, if you choose to provide it.
  • Your email address or other contact details when required for communication.
  • The situation, story, question, or other information you choose to submit.
  • Information included in a message sent through the Contact Us page.
  • Transaction-related information needed to identify or support a payment or refund.

You do not need to provide your name or location merely to share a situation unless the form specifically asks for information for a particular purpose.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Read and respond to your submitted situation.
  • Provide a Third Person Perspective.
  • Communicate with you about your request where necessary.
  • Respond to customer-support, refund, cancellation, or other enquiries.
  • Maintain records needed to operate the service and comply with applicable obligations.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, or security problems.

4. Personal Stories

Your story may contain personal or sensitive information because you choose what to share. Please share only information necessary for the perspective you want. Do not include passwords, card numbers, OTPs, Aadhaar numbers, or other highly sensitive credentials.

I THINK FOR YOU does not sell your personal information.

5. Payment Information

Where you make a voluntary contribution through a payment gateway, payment processing is handled by the applicable payment service provider. I THINK FOR YOU does not ask you to send card numbers, CVV numbers, OTPs, or banking passwords by email or through the story form.

6. Service Providers

We may use third-party services needed to operate the website, receive form submissions, communicate by email, or process payments. Such providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

7. Retention and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for legitimate business needs, or where required by law.

No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about information associated with your enquiry or to request correction or deletion where applicable and reasonably possible, subject to legal, operational, or record-keeping requirements.

9. Children

The website is not intended to be a service for children to independently submit sensitive personal situations. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us inappropriately, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page with its update date.
