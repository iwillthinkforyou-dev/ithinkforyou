The Idea

You know your story. But you may not see it from the outside.

When something matters deeply to us, our emotions, assumptions and fears can become part of the problem.

Sometimes what helps isn't another opinion from someone involved. It's simply another perspective.

Step Outside The Problem

I don't have to live your story to look at it with you.

You give me the context. I look at the situation from the outside, without being part of the relationships, emotions or history surrounding it.

A Human Perspective

Not AI. Not an algorithm. Not a chatbot.

Just a human being thinking about your situation.

I read what you share, try to understand the circumstances around it, and look at your situation from a different angle.

When useful, I may also research the place, culture, social environment or circumstances behind your story.

You Don't Have To Ask Someone You Know

Don't want to ask someone close to you?

That's okay.

Sometimes you don't want advice from someone who knows you, knows the other person, or might judge the situation.

Tell your story. You can remain anonymous.
