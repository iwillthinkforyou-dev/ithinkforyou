The idea

You know your story. But you may not see it from the outside.

When something matters deeply to us, our emotions, assumptions and fears can become part of the problem.

Sometimes what helps isn't another opinion from someone involved. It's simply another perspective.

Step outside the problem

I don't have to live your story to look at it with you.

You give me the context. I look at the situation from the outside, without being part of the relationships, emotions or history surrounding it.

How it works

Three simple steps.

01

Tell me

Tell me what happened, who's involved, what you're trying to understand and what you're worried about.

02

I think

I'll read your situation carefully and consider it from a third-person perspective.

03

You see another angle

I'll send you a thoughtful perspective that may help you see something you couldn't see from inside the situation.

What this is

Another way to think about what's happening.

  • A thoughtful outside perspective
  • A place to explain the whole situation
  • An opportunity to slow down and see the bigger picture
  • No judgement
  • No pretending to have all the answers
A small but important note

Perspective, not professional advice.

I THINK FOR YOU offers a personal perspective based on what you choose to share.

It isn't a replacement for professional medical, legal, financial or mental-health advice, and it isn't a crisis service.

You remain responsible for your own decisions.
