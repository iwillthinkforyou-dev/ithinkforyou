Why I Think For You

Sometimes, all we need is another human being to think with us.

We all get stuck inside our own thoughts.

When something matters deeply to us, emotions, fear, expectations and assumptions can make it difficult to see what's actually happening.

I'm a regular person, just like you.

I studied human communication and psychology, and I've always had a genuine interest in understanding people, situations and the way we think.

I believe that sometimes, the right perspective at the right time can save us from making a decision we'll spend years regretting.

That's why I think for you.

The idea

You bring the story.
I bring the distance.

You don't need someone to live your story to look at it with you. You give me the context. I step outside the situation and think about it without being part of the relationships, emotions or history surrounding it.

Why Third Person Perspective

You know the situation.
You don't always see the situation.

When we're inside a problem, we see it through our own emotions, history, fears and expectations.

That's why we naturally turn to other people.

But asking someone close to you isn't always easy. They may know the people involved. They may have their own opinions. You may be afraid of being judged — or simply afraid to tell them the whole story.

Sometimes, what you need isn't another opinion.

It's an outside perspective.

A person who doesn't know you, doesn't know the people involved, and isn't emotionally invested in the outcome.

Someone who can simply look at what you've told them and ask:

“What might this look like from the outside?”

That, for me, is a Third Person Perspective.

A Small But Important Note

Perspective, not professional advice.

I THINK FOR YOU isn't here to tell you what to do.

You already have your own mind, your own values and your own life to live.

Sometimes, you don't need someone to give you a solution. You just need someone to help you see the situation from another angle.

I THINK FOR YOU is not a replacement for a psychiatrist, psychologist, doctor, lawyer, financial advisor or other qualified professional.

It is not a crisis service.

It's simply one human being taking the time to think carefully about what another human being has shared.

You remain responsible for your own decisions.
