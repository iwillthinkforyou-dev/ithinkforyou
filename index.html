Why Third Person Perspective

Some things are easier to see when you're not inside them.

When we're part of a situation, our emotions, history and expectations become part of the way we see it.

That's why we often turn to people we trust.

But sometimes you don't want advice from someone who knows you, knows the other person, or already has an opinion.

You need someone outside the story.

Someone who isn't emotionally invested in the outcome and can simply ask:

“What might this look like from the outside?”

That's a Third Person Perspective.
